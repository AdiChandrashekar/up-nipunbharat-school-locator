# UP School Locator

A phone-first page for finding any of the 1,32,576 government schools in Uttar Pradesh's 75 districts (Nipun Bharat) and getting Google Maps directions to it.

**Live page:** https://adichandrashekar.github.io/up-nipunbharat-school-locator/

- Search by school name, UDISE code, block or district
- Filter by district, block or town, rural or urban, and whether coordinates are on record
- "Nearby" sorts schools by distance from your location
- One tap to Google Maps directions, share or copy coordinates

Data comes from the Nipun Plus app's public school directory (`nipunapi.prernaup.in`). Coordinates are as each school recorded them, so confirm on arrival.

Vibe-coded by [Aditya Chandrashekar](https://github.com/AdiChandrashekar).
